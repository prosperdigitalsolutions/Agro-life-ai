\# AGRO LIFE-AI Dataset Research



\## Purpose



The purpose of this document is to identify publicly available

agricultural datasets that can support the development and

evaluation of the AGRO LIFE-AI Biochemical Decay \& Shelf-Life

Predictor.



\## Current Development Dataset



The current 12-record synthetic dataset is used only for:



\- Software development

\- Data pipeline testing

\- Feature engineering testing

\- Machine-learning pipeline testing

\- Prototype interface testing



It is not presented as real agricultural measurement data.



\## Candidate Research Dataset 1



\### Tomato Shelf-Life and Quality Dataset



Source:

Dryad



Dataset:

Shelf life and quality of tomato (Lycopersicon esculentum Mill.)

fruits as affected by neem leaf extract dipping and beeswax coating.



DOI:

10.5061/dryad.5mkkwh772



Important variables include:



\- Storage period

\- Temperature

\- Relative humidity

\- Physiological loss in weight

\- Decay percentage

\- Marketability

\- pH

\- Titratable acidity

\- Total soluble solids

\- Ascorbic acid

\- Shelf life



Licence:

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.



Potential use:

Model development and evaluation after detailed inspection

of the dataset structure and suitability.





\## Candidate Research Dataset 2



\### Mango Supply-Chain and Fruit Quality Dataset



The dataset describes mango quality evolution during an

international air-cargo supply chain.



Important variables include:



\- Temperature

\- Relative humidity

\- Storage period

\- Mass loss

\- Peel colour

\- pH

\- Sugar content

\- Visual appearance



The dataset includes sensor measurements and quality

observations collected during transport and storage.



Potential use:

Investigation for temperature/humidity-based fruit quality

and shelf-life modelling.

\## Candidate Research Dataset 4



\### Commercial Apple Cold-Storage Dataset



The dataset contains time-series measurements from commercial

apple cold storage.



Important variables include:



\- Air temperature

\- Fruit surface temperature

\- Humidity

\- Dew point

\- Storage conditions



Licence:

Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.



Potential use:

Environmental-condition analysis and future sensor-based

extensions of AGRO LIFE-AI.





\## Dataset Selection Principle



A research dataset should only be incorporated into the

machine-learning model after:



1\. Dataset structure inspection

2\. Variable identification

3\. Target-variable identification

4\. Missing-value analysis

5\. Duplicate analysis

6\. Data-quality assessment

7\. Licence/access verification

8\. Compatibility assessment with AGRO LIFE-AI

9\. Appropriate preprocessing

10\. Academic documentation of the data source



\## Important Academic Note



The current synthetic dataset and the future research dataset

must remain clearly separated.



Synthetic development results must not be presented as

experimental agricultural findings.
